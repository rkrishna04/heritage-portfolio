01 The Story

A warm welcome,
not a résumé

In the tradition of the karigar — the artisan who pours quiet attention into every detail — I approach software as a living craft.

Lines of code are threads. Systems are looms. Every interface is a place where people and intention meet. I draw from Warli geometry, Madhubani storytelling, and temple jali rhythm — translating that spirit into digital products that feel considered and lasting.
