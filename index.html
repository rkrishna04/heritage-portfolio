The Story

A warm welcome, not a résumé

In the tradition of the karigar — the artisan who pours quiet attention into every detail — I approach software as a living craft. Lines of code are threads; systems are looms; every interface is a place where people and intention meet.

My work is rooted in clarity and care. I draw inspiration from the patient geometry of Warli, the storytelling of Madhubani, and the rhythmic borders of temple jali — translating that spirit into digital products that feel considered, human, and lasting.

The Craft

Karigari in the digital age

Philosophy shaped by traditional craftsmanship — patience, iteration, and respect for the material.

Attention to Detail

Like the fine lines of a Madhubani border, every pixel, transition, and edge case receives deliberate care. Craft is visible in the small things.

Iterative Rhythm

Build, observe, refine. The same cycle that turns a rough sketch into a finished painting guides the evolution of products and systems.

Material Honesty

Respect the medium. Code has its own grain. Interfaces should feel native to their platform while carrying a distinctive cultural voice.

Accessible by Design

Heritage is for everyone. Inclusive experiences are non-negotiable — from keyboard navigation to color contrast rooted in warm, legible palettes.
